## Freeze blockers and proposed amendment

See openq_A.md Q1–Q4. In particular, `InterventionPolicy.evaluate(assessments, alerts, cfg)` cannot distinguish an IN_PREP ticket from a COMPLETE ticket using the RiskAssessment fields specified in `15`. Adding lifecycle to that payload is a cross-device contract decision, not a syntax translation. Recommended proposal: add `ticket_lifecycle` to RiskAssessment and define how explicit human-resolution events reach AlertState through the existing AlertCommand contract. This proposal has NOT been implemented.

`EventLog.append(e: Event) -> Seq` accepts a committed envelope while EventSink says the log assigns the missing DraftEvent fields at append. A synchronous final Seq also needs a defined relationship to delayed reorder commitment. Recommended proposal: specify the draft-ingestion/reorder-drain boundary and injected time explicitly before implementing it. Do not silently add flush/drain methods absent from `22`.
